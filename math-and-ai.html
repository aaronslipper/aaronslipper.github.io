Response to the AHM statement

Aaron Slipper · October 2026

On October 7, 2026, Terence Tao published a guest post on his blog containing a statement by the Association for Human Mathematics (AHM) Communications Working Group about OpenAI’s October 6 release of mathematical documents. The statement expresses the AHM Working Group’s views and should not be attributed to Professor Tao.

I wrote to Professor Tao offering a rebuttal because I considered the statement mistaken and potentially damaging to the mathematical community. The essay below sets out my response to the AHM statement.
