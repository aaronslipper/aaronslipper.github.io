Aaron Slipper

Phillip Griffiths Assistant Research Professor
of Mathematics

I work in number theory and geometric representation theory, with particular interests in nonlinear Fourier transforms, the Langlands program, and logarithmic geometry.

I am at Duke University, where I work with Jayce Getz. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Chicago in 2024, advised by Ngô Bảo Châu and Kazuya Kato, and my B.A. from Harvard University in 2018.
