Number theory & geometric representation theory

Research

I study nonlinear Fourier transforms in the Braverman–Kazhdan program, Ngô’s Hankel transform program, and relative Langlands duality, especially their de Rham and étale categorifications. I also work on logarithmic geometry and its applications to geometric Langlands.
